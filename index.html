Who We Are

The Traditional Okinawa Goju Ryu Karate do Federation (TOGKF) is the guardian of the art as passed down to Higaonna Morio Sensei. We are not just a sport organization; we are a community dedicated to a way of life.

Our goal is to enrich every aspect of who you are through rigorous training and traditional values.

The Philosophy

"Traditional Okinawa Goju Ryu Karate-do is a challenging art. It will test you emotionally, mentally, and physically. But like anything in life the more you put into it, the more you will gain."
