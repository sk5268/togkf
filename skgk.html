Sanbo Ken Kai
Goju Ryu Karate-Do

Affiliated to TOGKF · Est. 2015

Sanbo Ken Kai Gojuryu was established in 2015 with a dedication to preserving and spreading traditional karate and the Higaonna Sensei system. Guided by our founder and chief instructor since day one, the dojo is built on a foundation of mutual respect, strict discipline, and continuous self-improvement.

We are a dedicated community where both children and adults train to cultivate mental focus, physical resilience, and strong character. By emphasizing the authentic techniques and core values of Gojuryu, our practice focuses on lifelong personal growth rather than just sport. Reflecting our commitment to these traditional roots, we have been proudly affiliated with TOGKF since 2023.

Sensei Vijayakumar M

Founder and Chief Instructor
4th Dan Karate Okinawa Goju Ryu TOGKF, Japan
2nd Dan Kobudo, Okinawa, Japan

Sensei Vijay Kumar M’s Karate-Do journey began in 1996 in Chennai. His foundational years were deeply shaped by his first teacher, Sempai Mohan (a student of Shihan M. Deivasigamani), and particularly by Sempai Balachandran, who dedicated extensive time to personally training him. During this time, he was highly praised by his seniors for his powerful and aggressive kicks, especially his mae-geri. Driven by this strong mentorship, he attended an International Training Camp under Grand Master Young Ku Yun in 1997. He achieved his Goju Ryu Shodan in 2000, having already earned his instructor’s license in 1999.

His capabilities quickly led to significant opportunities, including teaching his first school classes and an appointment as an Assistant Instructor training Indian Customs Officers in self-defense. He earned his Nidan in 2002 and his Sandan in 2013. Although he had moved to Hyderabad to build his professional career, he was more interested in practicing Karate, leading him to establish his own dojo there that same year. Nurturing a long-standing passion for traditional weaponry, Sensei Vijay also trained under Renshi Robert of Okinawan Shorin-Ryu Shorinkan, successfully achieving his Kobudo Shodan in 2017. To deeply understand Sanchin and Tensho, the core katas of Goju Ryu, Sensei Vijay also practices Yoga breathing techniques.

Throughout his career, he has held prominent leadership positions, such as the Chief Instructor of Telangana (joining Ohshikai Japan Goju Ryu India under Shihan Pratap Chand Katoch in 2014) and later serving as the South India Chief Incharge. It was his long-held dream to train under the legendary Shihan Morio Higaonna—a dream that was realized in 2023 when he joined TOGKF. He has additionally traveled to many countries to train under TOGKF Senior Instructors from Okinawa, Japan, and various other Goju Ryu teachers. Today, leading Sanbo Ken Kai, Sensei Vijay draws on his rich heritage and the immense dedication of his early mentors to guide his students along the authentic, holistic path of traditional Okinawan Goju Ryu.
